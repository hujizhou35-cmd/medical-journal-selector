## How the 100 + 50 experiment worked

**Development:** ten research classes, ten completed cases each. Cases passed through preparation, recommendation, two separate review contexts, reveal, diagnosis and an explicit change/no-change decision. Accepted rules were regression-tested. The 100 cases used evolving versions; they are not 100 tests of final V2.

**Final evaluation:** five cases per class, 50 in total. Frozen V1 and r18 V2 were compared with fixed keyword and TF-IDF rankings. V1/V2 received the same prepared evidence package; ranking baselines used the complete retrieval pool. This is controlled shared preparation, not four independent searches with identical access to every item.

**Masking and isolation:** preparation masked article/journal identity, authors and direct identifiers, while a separate answer store retained the historical outlet and label mappings. Generation and review contexts did not receive the answer maps. All four outputs were anonymized with controlled presentation order. Two fresh contexts of the same model (`gpt-6.1-sol`, xhigh) reviewed them; 40 cases had initial disagreements and all 40 received third-context adjudication. All 50 preparations, outputs and reviews were sealed before the whole-cohort reveal. Diagnoses followed without modifying r18.

This was AI review, not human-expert double-blind validation. Published-paper model memory and output-style leakage cannot be ruled out. Earlier masking repairs are disclosed. One reserve-selection order deviation and remaining hard failures prevented stable promotion. All 50 remain in the denominator, including poor recommendations, empty results and failures. These now-exposed cases can only support future regressions.

## V1 versus V2 on the same final 50

| Measure | V1 | V2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Historical outlet in the top ten | 4/50 (8%) | 1/50 (2%) |
| At least one usable recommendation, without retained hard failures | 14/50 (28%) | 16/50 (32%) |
| Cases affected by hard failures | 21/50 (42%) | 12/50 (24%) |
| Retained hard-failure flags | 37 | 41 |

Review recorded **11 V2 wins, 26 ties, 8 V1 wins and 5 unresolved cases**. V2 affected fewer cases with hard failures but retained more flags and matched fewer historical outlets. This does not establish a clear overall improvement. Historical-outlet matching is not acceptance probability.

## Tokens and time

The formal development-100 and final process record **100,980,112 input + 6,783,345 output = 107,763,457 tokens**. Usage is missing for 31 development calls and 5 final receipts, so this is not a complete total cost. Cached input and reasoning output are subsets and are not added again.

- The broader experiment observation span was about **3 days 4 hours 22 minutes**, including trials, regressions, checks and pauses.
- Nine complete final-wave intervals total about **7 hours 8 minutes** (25,696.83 seconds).
- The final-process observation span was about **13 hours 15 minutes**, including waiting, recovery and post-reveal diagnoses.

These describe different scopes, not interchangeable total runtimes. Summed concurrent call time is not elapsed wall time.
