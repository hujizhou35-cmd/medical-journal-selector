## The task and the outcome

The selector helps medical authors compare **Higher quartile / Time / Fit** submission routes using manuscript content and current journal evidence. It records source support, pending checks and exclusions. It does not predict acceptance or guarantee a successful submission. The [fictional example](../examples/fictional-report.md) illustrates the output, rather than a benchmark case.

We first completed **100 distinct valid development cases**, then evaluated a frozen candidate on **50 final cases** against the V1 baseline and two fixed ranking methods. These are case counts, **not 150 rule changes**. Many central decisions were no-change: a failure did not automatically justify a new universal rule.

| Final measure | V1: frozen baseline r1 | V2: frozen candidate r18 | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Historical publishing outlet in the top ten, Hit@10 | 4/50, 8% | 1/50, 2% | Lower for V2 on this measure |
| At least one usable recommendation, with no retained hard failure | 14/50, 28% | 16/50, 32% | Two more cases; not evidence of clear overall improvement |
| Cases affected by hard flags | 21/50 | 12/50 | Fewer affected cases, but unresolved V2 hard failures remain |
| Retained hard flags | 37 | 41 | More V2 flags; flag count differs from affected-case count |

The anonymous AI preference comparison gave **11 V2 wins, 26 ties, 8 V1 wins and 5 unresolved cases**. The record denominator is 50; decided win/tie/loss outcomes total 45. Unresolved is not tie. Only one case placed the historical outlet in both versions' top ten, with an equal exact rank. The 46 common non-hits are censored observations, not 46 proven exact-rank ties.

## Four methods, one final cohort

| Method | Hit@3 | Hit@5 | Hit@10 and Wilson 95% interval | Usable coverage | Hard-flag cases |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Fixed keyword ranking | 7/50 | 10/50 | 17/50, 34% [22.4%, 47.8%] | 20/50 | 0/50 |
| Fixed abstract TF-IDF ranking | 6/50 | 10/50 | 12/50, 24% [14.3%, 37.4%] | 20/50 | 1/50 |
| V1 complete report | 4/50 | 4/50 | 4/50, 8% [3.2%, 18.8%] | 14/50 | 21/50 |
| V2 complete report | 1/50 | 1/50 | 1/50, 2% [0.4%, 10.5%] | 16/50 | 12/50 |

Keyword and TF-IDF comparators rank content similarity. V1/V2 also assess current article admission, applicable method policy, author requirements and supporting evidence. Ranking comparators do not claim complete evidence reports. Both use shared model-assisted research concepts and abstract preparation, rather than unmasked author text alone, so they are not wholly model-independent baselines.

A historical publishing outlet is one known outlet, **not an acceptance probability or the uniquely correct journal**. A non-hit is not automatically a recommendation error: current policy or missing evidence may justify withholding a candidate. Usable means at least one ranked candidate supported by effective review and no retained hard failure for that result. Discovery, rank and usable coverage remain separate.

## What counted as a development loop

**Prepare and seal → recommend → two separate blind review contexts → reveal → diagnose → explicit central decision.** Disagreement could require adjudication. An accepted change needed its actual regressions before the next version was frozen. A completed profile, selection or model call did not by itself complete a case.

Ten class queues supplied at most one original manuscript per unfinished class per wave. Earlier runs genuinely reached ten concurrent model calls; later development used a **five-call cap** after transport failures. Queue count, configured workers and actual concurrent calls are distinct. The registered scheduler advanced continuously; the one-minute fallback observer did not impose a delay between roles. Running inputs and rules stayed frozen, and central decisions waited for the whole-wave barrier.

## What changed between the baseline and r18

V1 already required current evidence, three routes, explicit unknowns and hard constraints. Frozen r18 clarified several boundaries rather than introducing those principles for the first time.

| Change | Problem it addresses |
|---|---|
| Match policy triggers to the actual study | Separate design, database provenance and access conditions; do not turn a routing label into a policy definition or an illustrative validation list into an exhaustive one |
| Separate source type from permitted use | A journal-registry record can support title/ISSN identity; an ordinary paper record cannot establish current article admission, indexing or metrics |
| Audit the final serialized evidence | Recheck captured URLs, literal quotations and timestamps in the delivered file, not only working notes |
| Support both sides of precedent comparisons | Verify manuscript and comparator evidence before relative claims; leave the comparison unknown when either side is unsupported |
| Check delegated specialty-section admission | Journal-level permission does not prove the selected section accepts the article; keep unresolved permission pending without inventing scientific gates |
| Specify timeline endpoints and user language | Separate first decision, acceptance, publication, online and indexing; follow the user's language with explicit English unknown markers |

Inspect the [actual Skill](../../skills/medical-journal-selector/SKILL.md), [method guidance](../../skills/medical-journal-selector/references/medical-methods.md), [source guidance](../../skills/medical-journal-selector/references/sources-and-verification.md) and [evidence format](../../skills/medical-journal-selector/references/evidence-format.md).

Execution repairs separately addressed same-study registration filtering, full-name masking, floated tables/captions, official retrieval paths and bounded stop/resume behavior. The r31 software fallback handles only balanced, attribute-free `i`, `b`, `em` and `strong` formatting within a captured bibliography-precedent line; it does not weaken content or official-policy source requirements. [Bounded formatting regression](https://github.com/hujizhou35-cmd/medical-journal-selector/blob/0f9ffb7adbad863643428475412252e7ca322a42/docs/evaluation/regressions/r31-inline-matcher.json)

Software checks and exposed-input regressions establish behavior within their checked scope. **They add no ordinary blind cases, rewrite no original scores and demonstrate no accuracy increase.** The experimental package retains the frozen r18 software. A later code or instruction revision cannot inherit this final cohort's validation result.

## How the final comparisons were isolated

Each case had shared study preparation, retrieval windows/budgets and source snapshots. The fixed keyword/TF-IDF rankings and comparison configuration were sealed before recommendation. V1 and V2 received the same delivered package; fixed baselines used the whole retrieved pool. This measures decisions on controlled preparation, **not four independent autonomous searches or identical access to every retrieved item**.

The four outputs were anonymized with controlled presentation orders. Two fresh, separate review contexts used the existing authenticated **gpt-6.1-sol / xhigh**, with a third adjudication context where necessary. Initial field or pairwise disagreements occurred in 40/50 cases; all 40 received adjudication. Every final case's preparation, generation and reviews were sealed before any final answer reveal. Genuine diagnoses and explicit decisions followed the whole-cohort reveal without changing r18. The final 50 are now exposed: they can support future regressions, not a new blind evaluation.

The historical outlet appeared in the full retrieved pool for **27/50** cases, in the delivered package for **17/50**, and was actually assessed by each complete-report method for **16/50**. These are different coverage stages, not accuracy estimates. Post-reveal non-hit analysis records the earliest supported blocking stage; an empty ranking does not prove a ranking error, and an earlier recovered connection failure does not by itself explain a final non-hit. [Coverage](https://github.com/hujizhou35-cmd/medical-journal-selector/blob/0f9ffb7adbad863643428475412252e7ca322a42/docs/evaluation/final50/public_coverage_rank_summary.json) · [Non-hit classification](https://github.com/hujizhou35-cmd/medical-journal-selector/blob/0f9ffb7adbad863643428475412252e7ca322a42/docs/evaluation/final50/public_nonhit_classification.json)

## Why the stable gates did not pass

**The strict protocol gate is GATES_NOT_PASSED.** `laboratory-017` was selected without a preselection basis for skipping the earlier `laboratory-010` and `laboratory-016` reserves. This historical deviation cannot be made compliant after the fact. All 50 cases, five per class, their original results and attempts remain included. A filtered 49-case cohort does not replace the primary result.

V2 still has hard flags in 12/50 cases, so the zero-unresolved-final-V2-hard-failure condition was not met. Passing wins-at-least-losses or slightly exceeding V1 usable coverage alone does not pass the entire gate. Overlapping intervals do not demonstrate significant improvement. V2's 41 retained flags contain 39 exact within-case signatures and two repeats; V1 has 37/37. Exact signatures do not establish distinct causal errors, and two reviewers flagging the same claim is not two independent mistakes.

Additional limits remain visible:

- The stratified sample uses publicly obtainable papers, rather than all medical submissions. Public-paper model memory cannot be ruled out; earlier masking amendments and preparation defects are disclosed.
- The post-reveal diagnosis did not confirm the original primary class of `public_database-011`; its original class, score and primary denominator remain, without outcome-based reassignment.
- Separate contexts still use the same model. AI blind review is not independent human medical-expert validation, and output style can reveal comparator type.
- All 50 package labels say `as_of=2026-10-02`, while actual source captures occurred on 4 October UTC. Source data dates were not systematically recovered. This is not historical-cutoff validation or evidence that changing fields remain valid indefinitely. [Source-time metadata](https://github.com/hujizhou35-cmd/medical-journal-selector/blob/0f9ffb7adbad863643428475412252e7ca322a42/docs/evaluation/final50/public_source_time_metadata.json)
- Generator-labelled `verified` fields record assertions, not independent proof. Unified independent SCIE/JCR confirmation coverage is unknown; PubMed does not establish SCIE.
- Two optional opaque pre-diagnosis score-snapshot attempts were legitimately rejected after their capture window. There is no independent historical before/after score-byte comparison to invent; current seals, source-code behavior and hashes retain their narrower evidentiary scope.
